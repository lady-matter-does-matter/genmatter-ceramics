GENEVIEVE MATTER
ABOUT

Genevieve Matter (b. 2000) is a South African artist raised in Durban and based in Johannesburg, where she completed her Fine Arts degree. Her primary practice is in ceramics, though she does dabble in the art of printmaking, as well as multimedia installations.

Matter throws and configures vessels on a wheel and decorates her vessels with gestural intuitive mark-making that mirrors her emotional and mental states. Each piece becomes an extension of herself — a glimpse into her inner landscape of curiosities, traumas, and infatuations. She finds the physical act of working with clay grounding and primal, describing ceramics as a continual process of learning, surrender, and renewal.

Matter’s work is deeply informed by the philosophy of the vessel — its histories, implications, and holdings. She is drawn to the notion that for something to be held within a space, a boundary must exist — a wall that defines what is inside and what remains outside. This binary, though divided, is also united by one of the vessel’s most defining features: the rim, the meeting edge or threshold where containment meets openness. She links these ideas to her creative process which draws from feminine cycles, natural rhythms, and ancestral lineage — reaching toward her “creative mothers and fathers.” For Matter, art-making is not a pursuit to be achieved but a vital, regulatory act of being.
