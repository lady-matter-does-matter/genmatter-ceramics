GENEVIEVE MATTER
CERAMICS

Genevieve Matter created these particular works as immediate, momentary expressions of herself, through rough, less-than-thought-out doodles carved into the raw surface of a pot. She enjoys the meticulous nature of throwing; it allows her to create a canvas with a curve, formed quickly with the pull of her thumb and forefinger. This permits her to focus on her mark-making and what she is willing to portray in each piece, centering on curve, balance, colour, and shape.

Her drawing process is intimate and vulnerable. With each vessel, she opens herself to critique and advice, growing through repetition and imperfection. The images are fuelled by her most recent experiences, inspired by what she feels passionate about. Art, to her, is a life force, and these expressive marks are a reflection of that. Sometimes they are made with slow, uneasy scrapes, and sometimes it’s a sure, clean line.

Colour combinations are chosen intuitively, by the preference of what she feels looks good together. She creates with the understanding that anything can happen within the 1000+ degree firing — submitting to the unknown and unpredictability of such an extreme environment in which her precious vessels are exposed.

The bowls can hold whatever you choose to fill them with, whilst the outside is left raw and unglazed — an ode to the binaries of experience: finished and unfinished, clean and marked, smooth and gritty.
